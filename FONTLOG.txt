FONTLOG for Sagara Balinese

This file provides detailed information on the Sagara Balinese font software.
This information should be distributed along with the Sagara Balinese fonts and any derivative works.

Basic Font Information
Sagara Balinese is a display typeface designed specifically for the fashion and beauty industry, featuring a harmonized combination of Latin and Balinese scripts. The design process focuses on high contrast and elegant curves to suit contemporary branding needs while preserving cultural heritage.

Created by type designer Ni Putu Rahayu Satriani (ISI Denpasar internship program) under the art direction of Balibilly Design.
